# FE14 weapon Chinese translation review 06 — Swords (revised English pass)

This replaces review 05. English has first been revised against the Fandom list and the captured Serenes inventory effects:

- effective-damage wording is now `Triples might against`;
- Silver weapons retain `a stacking debuff of` and specify the one-point-per-turn recovery;
- every verified numeric follow-up modifier uses `effective Speed` (including special and DLC weapons); and
- where the English source has no descriptor, the proposed Chinese description is deliberately blank.

These Chinese descriptions are proposed only and are **not yet included in runtime data**.

## Availability Decision Needed: Water Splash

| English | Japanese source name | Proposed Simplified Chinese name | Proposed Simplified Chinese description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water Splash | 水の飛沫 | 水之飞沫 | 魔法武器，以魔力与魔防计算伤害；无法追击；无法与他人交换（可调整行动顺序）。 |

The Japanese source does not mark Water Splash as enemy-only, but it is listed under `Other` rather than Dragonstones or Beaststones. By user approval, include it after the Dragonstone and Beaststone sections as an `Other player-available` Stones subsection.

## Review Notes

- Explicitly enemy-only breath, fist, rock, and saw rows carry `☆` in the Japanese source and are not included in this translation batch.
- `真龙石`, `守兽石`, `超兽石`, and `水之飞沫` are literal project translations, not sourced official Chinese localizations.

## Review Notes

- Existing project character translations were retained for `艾丽泽`, `樱`, and `浅间`.
- The user requested category suffixes `杖` and `祓串` be removed from translated display names. Mechanical family remains separate normalized data.
- `彩虹桥`, `拖拽`, `七难即灭`, `神风招来`, and `祸事罪秽` are literal project translations, not sourced official Chinese localizations.
- Astral Blessing is omitted because the Japanese source marks it as player-unobtainable. It remains in the source-inventory report for audit.
